HOW EIC INVEST

We buy and invest in good, profitable SMEs — and back turnarounds when good companies get a little stuck

Finance or investment?

EIC operates within a network of professional investors and commercial lenders. We can arrange finance or investment for just about any purpose.

Selling your company?

Our team takes time to understand where our perspective or expertise can best add value, and we focus on empowering management teams to build the best business in their market — whether you're funding retirement, raising capital for a new investment, bringing in fresh experience, or moving on to a new challenge, we'll plan a way forward that's right for you and the business.

Getting your company ready for sale?

You don't make money running a business — you make money when you sell it. A common trap is getting caught up working in the business, not on it. We're not focused on any one industry; as long as we see a viable route to success, your company could be mainstream or niche and still be our starting point.

CUSTOMER ACQUISITION & RETENTION

Our dedicated, in-house customer acquisition and retention team works closely with leadership teams to build efficient, data-driven acquisition engines — treating marketing as a profit centre, not a cost.
